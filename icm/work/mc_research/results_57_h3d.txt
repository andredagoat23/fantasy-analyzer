    labelled: designed AFTER seeing section 4. The primary endpoint
    verdict above stands unchanged; this section explains it.]

9. NOT DONE / LIMITATIONS (stated, per charter)
==============================================================================
   - No lead-time measurement, no mechanism gate (H3a), no change-point
     detector (H3b), no earned/vacated split (H3c) — post-draft WS3.
   - No lineup-slot gating (charter 7.10): this harness carries no
     roster state; alerts are position-level. The machinery accepts a
     per-week startability gate when WS3 proper builds roster context.
   - No waiver/acquisition friction (S9): precision/recall is the honest
     instrument per the charter's corrected WS3 benchmark; points-per-
     season from acting on alerts is NOT claimed anywhere above.
   - snap_share_step operates on a reduced universe (offense_pct join
     gaps: 23% of 2015 rows null). Its recall is disadvantaged; its
     precision is comparable (alerts drawn where the stat exists).
   - QB excluded from the arena (blacklist is a skill-position error;
     QB tier changes are QB-change events, not usage steps). K/DST have
     no league-scored history in the panel (46_ scope cut).
   - Events are defined on the DETECTABLE universe (4+ played games by
     W, W in 4..12): late-season (W>12) and week-1-3 tier changes are
     out of scope; the W floor exists because a 2-week window needs a
     baseline, the cap so every event has >= 4 outcome weeks.
   - Volume gates (8 carries / 8 touches in-window) were fixed a priori
     and not swept; a gate sweep is a different hypothesis (it would
     measure the GATE, which section 4b shows is the active ingredient).
   - mult/lift currencies appear nowhere; nothing here re-ranks a draft
     board. This is detector instrumentation, not a drafting rule (C3).
   - FDR bookkeeping: this script contributes exactly 1 primary endpoint
     to the charter-wide Benjamini-Hochberg count (S14).
